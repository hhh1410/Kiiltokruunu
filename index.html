Why Kiiltokruunu

Why Choose a Professional

Clean windows change the feel of a home more than most people realize. Light passes through unobstructed, views open up as they should, and the home looks cared for — both from outside and within. The same is noticeable from the outside too: visitors, neighbors, and passersby register clean windows subconsciously as part of a well-kept home, even if they couldn't say exactly where the impression comes from.

Still, window washing is one of those household tasks that's easy to put off. High windows, awkward corners, the lack of the right tools, and simply not having time make the job both slow and, often, a little risky — especially when it comes to second-floor windows or balcony glass.

We take care of it for you. Kiiltokruunu provides professional window cleaning for homes across the Helsinki metropolitan area — no chemical residue, no streaks, and no extra effort on your part. The work is done with the right tools, the right technique, and the care that comes from years of experience. We don't compromise on the result — a window is either clean, or we're not done yet.

Reliability matters to us as much as the result itself. An agreed time is kept, the job is finished properly, and the result is something you can be proud of weeks later. We don't leave work half-done, and we don't rush through it — we do it right the first time, so you don't have to think about it again for a long while. Many of our customers tell us the biggest reason they book the service isn't just clean windows — it's not having to think about it at all.

Professionalism also shows in what we don't do: we don't leave drips on the frames, we don't miss the corners, and we don't leave your yard or balcony messier than it was before we arrived. A small detail, but it's exactly what separates careful work from a quick job.

Weather in the Helsinki area tests windows year-round. Winter's salty slush, spring pollen, and autumn's steady rain leave their mark on glass faster than most people expect. Regular, professional cleaning isn't just a one-off tidying task — it's a way of keeping your home in good shape year after year, without you having to climb a ladder or dig the right tools out of a closet.

What's Included

Our core service is washing windows from the outside, and that's what most of our customers choose. We clean the glass, frames, and seals carefully, so the result is visible immediately — not just for a moment. Outdoor grime — dust, pollen, rain, and exhaust — builds up slowly but steadily, which is exactly why the result is often more dramatic than the time the job takes would suggest.

The service also includes interior washing and cleaning between window panes when agreed on in advance, at the quote stage. In many homes, the space between the panes has gone untouched for years — there too, the difference before and after is significant.

Balcony glass and sliding doors are one of our specialties. Tracks, railings, and glass surfaces are cleaned so the whole balcony feels fresh, not just the glass itself. Sliding balcony glass tracks often collect sand and grit, which makes sliding harder and shows up as streaks on the glass — we take care of that as part of the same visit. Tighter corners and harder-to-reach surfaces in apartment buildings are also handled without extra fuss or a separate trip.

High windows aren't a problem. We use specialized equipment that reaches up to six meters, letting us safely reach second-floor windows and tall fixed panes — no ladders on the roof, no unnecessary risk. That means we can wash every window on a building's facade in a single visit, regardless of which floor they're on.

Every home is different, so we don't offer one fixed package for everyone. Instead, we go through your windows at the quote stage and clearly explain what the work includes and what it costs, before anything begins. That way there are no surprises — in price or in the result.

Our tools and cleaning agents are chosen to suit the job, not the lowest price. We use clean water and effective, surface-friendly cleaning agents that don't leave chemical residue on glass or frame seals. The squeegee technique that leaves glass streak-free takes practice — and that's exactly the difference between a DIY attempt and a professional finish. The same applies to finishing corners and frames: that's where sloppy work shows first.

The service suits detached houses, townhouses, and apartments alike. In detached houses, we often wash every window on the facade in one visit; in townhouses, we typically focus on the front- and back-facing windows plus the terrace glass; in apartments, balcony glass and high windows are usually the part residents can't safely handle themselves. Regardless of the property, the starting point is the same: a clear quote, an agreed schedule, and a result that holds up to a closer look.

How We Ensure Quality, Speed, and Safety

Requesting a quote is quick: tell us the number of windows and your address, and you'll get a clear price estimate without a long wait. Once a time is agreed, we arrive as scheduled. You don't need to plan your day around ours — it's enough that we can get in, after which you're free to go about your business. A typical home takes 1–3 hours depending on the number of windows, and we'll give you an estimate for that too, right at the quote stage.

Safety is part of professionalism, not an add-on. We use proper equipment for high spots and make sure no mess is left in the yard, on the balcony, or indoors. Insured work means you can trust that nothing is left to chance — for your sake and ours. We don't climb onto roofs and we don't take risks that good equipment makes unnecessary — instead of ladders, we use platforms and extendable poles that keep both the person doing the work and your home safe.

Quality shows in the details. We don't just wash the center of the glass — we clean the whole surface, frames and corners included. Once the job is done, we walk through the result together if needed, and if anything falls short, we fix it before we leave. That's also why we offer a satisfaction guarantee: you pay only once you're happy with the result.

Communication is part of the service, just like the cleaning itself. If the schedule changes or something is unclear, you can reach us easily — we won't leave you wondering where things stand or when we're coming. The same goes for the quote stage: we answer questions before anything is agreed, so the decision is easy to make.

Pricing is straightforward and based on window size — small, medium, and large windows are priced separately. We always give the exact estimate on site, though, since we can't see your home in advance, and the actual size and position of your windows affect the scope of the work. The home renovation tax deduction (kotitalousvähennys) is always taken into account, so the real cost is often lower than the price list first suggests.

Clean windows aren't a luxury — they're part of a home that looks cared for and feels good to be in. Let us take care of it for you, reliably and with care, time after time.
